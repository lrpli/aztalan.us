Discover Aztalan

A Place Where Ancient
History Lives

Aztalan State Park preserves one of Wisconsin's most remarkable archaeological treasures — a Mississippian culture site occupied roughly 900–1300 CE, with connections stretching from the Great Lakes to the Gulf Coast.

⛰️

The Mounds

Three major platform mounds dominate the landscape — earthen monuments built by hand over centuries. These structures served as ceremonial platforms, elite residences, and symbols of political power in the Mississippian world.

Learn More
🏛️

The Stockade

A reconstructed wooden palisade wall with bastions encircles much of the ancient settlement. Built in 1968 using original post holes, it evokes the defensive architecture that once protected this thriving community on the banks of the Crawfish River.

Our Mission

The Friends of Aztalan State Park Inc. exists to support, assist, and promote interpretive, scientific, historical, and educational visitor services — ensuring this irreplaceable landmark endures for generations to come.

About the Friends Group

Dedicated to Preserving
Wisconsin's Ancient Heritage

Friends of Aztalan State Park Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization organized for the charitable and educational purpose of supporting, assisting, and promoting visitor services at this extraordinary site.

We fund programs, improvements, and educational initiatives that the state park system alone cannot provide — from archaeological research to school group tours to our ambitious new Visitor Center campaign.

🏺
National Historic Landmark since 1964 — one of Wisconsin's most important archaeological sites, with connections to the broader Mississippian world centered at Cahokia.
📚
Educational programs — group tours for adults and school children, an annual newsletter, and funded archaeological research keep Aztalan's story alive.
🌱
Visitor Center campaign — working toward a $1 million goal to build a world-class interpretive center, with $400,000 already raised.
