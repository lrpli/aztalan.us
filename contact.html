📌 Directions to the Park

Physical address: N6200 County Highway Q, Lake Mills, WI 53551

Aztalan State Park is located approximately 3 miles east of Lake Mills, Wisconsin, off County Highway Q. From Interstate 94, take Exit 267 toward Lake Mills, then follow County Q east approximately 1.5 miles to the park entrance on the right.

The park is approximately 40 miles east of Madison and 60 miles west of Milwaukee — accessible from both the Madison and Milwaukee metro areas.

Plan Your Visit

Park Hours & Visitor Info

Aztalan State Park is open to visitors year-round. Hours vary by season. A Wisconsin State Park vehicle admission sticker is required. Please check the Wisconsin DNR website for the most current information before your visit.

🌞

Park Hours (General)

Daily (Spring/Summer/Fall) 6 AM – 11 PM
Winter (Dec – Feb) 6 AM – 8 PM

Hours subject to change. Always check Wisconsin DNR for current park hours and any closures.

🚗

Admission

Wisconsin Residents (Annual) State Park Sticker
Non-Residents State Park Sticker
Daily Pass Available At Entrance

A valid Wisconsin State Park vehicle admission sticker is required. Purchase at the park or through the Wisconsin DNR website.

🌡️

Best Times to Visit

Spring (Apr–May) Wildflowers, quiet trails
Summer (Jun–Aug) Events & full programming
Fall (Sep–Nov) Foliage, dramatic skies

The park is beautiful in every season. Summer offers the most programming; fall offers stunning views of the mounds against the Wisconsin landscape.
